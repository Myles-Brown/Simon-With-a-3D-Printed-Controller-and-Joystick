# Print Oriantation
For the best quality prints the top of of the controllor should be printed with the outside of the controllor facing up.
The bottom pice of the controllor should be printed with its outside touching the build plate so the inside of the controllor is not deformed.

# Controllor Mounting modifications
to keep the joystick connected to four pillars are used to slot through the four holes on the HW-504 joystick module. 
However depending on the exact joystick you use or the vendor the dimensions of the holes on the joystick may slightly vary so it is recommended that 
if a 3d model of the joystick of choice is available you modify the joystick mounting for an exact fit.
